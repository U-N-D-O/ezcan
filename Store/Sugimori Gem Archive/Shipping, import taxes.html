1. Core Principle: 0% Seller Profit on Shipping

All shipping fees charged to buyers are 100% pure pass-through costs. The total amount covers:

  • Tracked & Insured Postage (Tusass): Base airmail letter + mandatory value insurance (Nalillit) scaling directly with card value.
  • eBay Final Value Fees: eBay automatically takes ~13-15% in fees directly from the shipping cost paid by the buyer at checkout.
  • Customs Declaration: Every package is officially declared with its exact total sales value to ensure absolute legal and customs safety.

🛡️🔒 ULTIMATE SECURE PACKAGING & 100% FULL INSURANCE 🔒🛡️

Your investment is protected from the moment it leaves Nuuk. Every single order is 100% fully insured for its exact total sales value and packed meticulously using a safe multi-layer method:

3. Destination Guidelines: USA vs. Europe

🇺🇸 Shipping to the USA

  • Tariffs / Duties: Greenland is outside the EU. Trading cards (HS Code 9504.40.00) manufactured in Japan and shipped from Greenland carry 0% US Import Duty.
  • De Minimis Limit ($800): Shipments under $800 USD clear US customs smoothly with zero paperwork for the buyer.
  • $800 - $2,500 (Informal Entry): 0% duty on cards, but USPS or couriers may apply minor handling fees.
  • Local Sales Tax: Automatically calculated, collected, and remitted directly by eBay at checkout based on the buyer's state.

🇪🇺 Shipping to Europe (EU)

  • Status: Greenland is an autonomous territory and NOT part of the EU customs union.
  • Import VAT: EU buyers must pay local national import VAT (e.g., 20-25%) on imports from outside the EU.
  • IOSS (Import One-Stop Shop): eBay collects EU VAT at checkout for items under €150 for smooth customs handling. For items >€150, local postal carriers collect VAT + handling fees upon delivery.
